## Who we are

THG Services, LLC does business as THG AI Services. We are a software company based in Woodstock, Georgia, helping local businesses across Cherokee County and metro Atlanta.

The founder is David Brannan. He has 25 years of experience running multiple seven-figure, multi-state businesses, and he uses technology to run his own operations and cut the busywork between a problem and the result. Every app we build started with a problem he hit as an operator first.

Our mission, in plain words: we help local small businesses use AI so their businesses run better and owners get more time with their families.

We design, build, and support our own products, from the first idea through onboarding and long-term support. We don't white-label other people's software.

## What we do, in plain words

We sit down with local business owners, learn how their day actually runs, and fix the busywork with the right mix of software and AI tools. Services:

- Call handling: we set up your phone line with after-hours coverage, smart call routing, and lead capture from inbound calls.
- AI Website Concierge: a chat helper on your website that answers questions, can read its answers aloud, captures leads around the clock, and sets up callbacks.
- Google Ads management: campaign setup, audits, and ongoing optimization, built on 15+ years of AdWords experience, including running campaigns across multi-state operations.
- SEO: on-page fixes, local search and your Google Business Profile, and content that targets the services you actually sell.
- Website grade check: a review of your current site covering search readiness, mobile, speed signals, tracking, and lead capture, with a plain-language fix list.
- Website upgrades and new builds: we modernize older sites, fix places where visitors drop off, and build landing pages for ads.
- Analytics and tracking: we set up Google Analytics and conversion tracking so you can see where your leads come from.
- Reviews and reputation: review requests sent at the right moment, and keeping your Google profile current.
- Email and inbox automation: we automate sorting, response templates, escalation rules, and shared-inbox flows so nothing gets dropped. We also build internal tools and integrations much faster than traditional development with AI coding tools, write custom scripts and automation that connect your tools and generate the reports nobody wants to build by hand, connect QuickBooks or Xero to faster profit and loss dashboards that show margin and cash position in close to real time, and handle bookkeeping cleanup: cleaning up the books, setting up better categories, and making life easier for your accountant.

If a visitor's problem isn't on this list, they can tell us about it and we'll figure out what tooling or process fixes it.

## How working with us goes

It starts with a quick call to hear what is eating up your time. Then we meet with you online, by video, and map out how your day really runs: calls, leads, estimates, email, ads, and books. From there we pick the smallest mix of software and AI tools that fixes the busywork, and we use what you already have wherever we can. We set it up, connect it, and show your team how it works. After that we stay on as support and tune things as your business changes. On that first call the team gives you a straight answer about scope and cost.

## After-hours calls

When a customer calls after hours, the call gets answered instead of going to voicemail. The caller hears a friendly greeting and says what they need, and the system takes down their name, their number, and the job. Urgent calls can be routed to whoever is on call. Everything else becomes a message with a callback request. You get the details by text or email, so nothing sits in a voicemail box.
